The project introduces To-Do, a web-based to-do list management system by Spring Boot environment. The aim of this project is to make life easier for a variety range of people – students, workers, or just some ordinary people – as long as they have something to do in their mind, they are a fit to this application. The application proposes beyond the values of only organizing tasks and making our users’ lives easier but also providing visual motivation for users to improve living standards. I believe with the help of To-Do, people will easily manage tedious yet necessary tasks during lives, while enjoying them by looking at the status going up, thus raising their level of discipline, which results in efficient yet stress-free high-quality lives.
